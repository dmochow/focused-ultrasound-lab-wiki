## This document describes the objectives of Rhiana's research

## End-goal
Learn how to conduct experiments combining:
    - the animal
    - fiber optic cable connected to the animal
    - Doric Neuroscience Studio capturing neural data and events
    - ABET software running the schedule and issuing pulses when events occur 


## SKills you need to acquire:
    - How to operate Doric Neuroscience Studio
        - configuration that allows us to capture DA fluorescence and events
    - How to connect the animal to the fiber optic cable + Doric cube
    - How to program a desired schedule into ABET
    - How to collect FP + behavioral data with animal in the chamber
